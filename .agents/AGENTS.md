# Project-Scoped Guidelines - Ansar Family (Kerala, India)

## Context & Localization Rules
- **Target Audience**: Local Muslim community / Mahal committee in Kerala, India.
- **Currency**: Indian Rupee (`INR` / `₹`). Never use `USD` or `$`.
- **Phone Numbers**: Indian format starting with `+91` (e.g. `+91 94470 12345`).
- **Addresses**: Kerala regional address formats (e.g. `Juma Masjid Road, Malappuram, Kerala 676505`).
- **Terminology**: Respect local Mahallu community terms (e.g. `Mahallu Welfare Fund`, `Baithul Maal`, `Sadaqah & Zakat`).
